 Racial and Economic Disparities Analysis in Food Accessibility

Team Leader

May 2024

I led a team that conducted an extensive analysis of food accessibility disparities across 72,532 U.S. census tracts using USDA’s Food Access Research Atlas. Reduced complexity by cleaning and consolidating 147 variables by 60%, and identified significant correlations (0.53) between African American populations and poverty rates in relation to supermarket accessibility. Enhanced model precision (R² from 0.19 to 0.24) by integrating state GDP data, and visualized critical patterns using scatter plots, heatmaps, and regression analysis to illustrate systemic inequalities clearly. Critically evaluated the study’s limitations and proposed future research directions involving longitudinal and qualitative approaches to inform targeted policy interventions.

 Comparative Rent Price Analysis Across BU Neighborhoods

Team Leader

Dec 2022

I led a team in conducting a comparative analysis of apartment rent prices in four neighborhoods near Boston University. We analyzed cross-sectional data to examine how variables like room size, year built, bed types, days on market, crime rate, and average income influence rent prices. The analysis revealed significant positive correlations between factors such as year built, bed types, and room size with rent prices, providing valuable insights into the housing market dynamics near BU.
